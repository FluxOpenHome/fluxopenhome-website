Design

A dashboard as beautiful as your home

Drag, drop, and place anything on a free-form canvas. Buttons, gauges, graphs, thermostats, cameras, floor plans — sized and styled exactly how you want.

  • Per-device layouts for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV
  • Real snap-to-grid and smart alignment
  • Dozens of control styles, gauges, and tile shapes
Living Artwork

Art that reacts to your home

Map a library of premium vector objects to your entities and watch them come alive — lights glow in their real color, fans and washers spin, thermometers fill, tanks show their level, and you can dial in the exact animation speed.

  • Lamps, fans, appliances, vehicles, water, outdoor & wellness gear
  • Grills, smokers, fire pits, EV chargers, hot tubs, saunas & more
  • Import your own SVGs and wire them up in seconds
Insight

Real history, plotted beautifully

Flux pulls your actual Home Assistant history and renders smooth, multi-entity charts with live readouts — temperature, energy, solar, anything numeric — over the range you choose.

  • Live values and flexible time windows
  • Full metric / imperial unit conversion everywhere
  • Gauges, energy flow, and weather tiles too
Automate

Automations that branch — and explain themselves

A visual “When → Only if → Do” builder that reads like plain English, with branching Cases for different situations. Then test it, run it, and trace exactly where it succeeded or failed.

  • Triggers, conditions, and a deep action library
  • Nestable Cases — do different things in different situations
  • Built-in testing, monitoring, and step-by-step traces
