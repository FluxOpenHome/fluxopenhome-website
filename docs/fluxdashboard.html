Built for iPad

The big canvas your home deserves

The iPad turns Flux into a design studio — a wide, free-form canvas with a tailored layout that's all its own, perfect on the desk or mounted on the wall.

  • A dedicated iPad layout, designed independently from iPhone and Apple TV
  • Drag-and-drop builder with snap-to-grid, smart alignment and free zoom
  • Wall-panel kiosk mode with a clock screensaver and wake-on-motion
Living Artwork & Floor Plans

Art — and a floor plan — that react to your home

Draw your home's floor plan with walls, doors and furniture, then map a library of premium vector objects to your entities and watch everything come alive — lights glow in their real color, fans and washers spin, thermometers fill, tanks show their level.

  • To-scale floor plans you can set as a live dashboard backdrop
  • Lamps, fans, appliances, vehicles, water, outdoor & wellness gear
  • Import your own SVGs and wire them up in seconds
Apple, Deeply Integrated

Your whole Apple home, in one place

Flux speaks fluent Apple. Control your Apple Home accessories right beside Home Assistant, play Apple Music across every speaker, and pull in Apple Weather and your calendars — natively, with no extra setup.

  • Apple Home — show and control HomeKit accessories alongside Home Assistant
  • Apple Music — play to AirPlay 2 speakers, group rooms, scrub the track, see album art & visualizers
  • Apple Weather — full daily & hourly forecasts in your units
  • Helpers — build virtual devices that combine and calculate, in plain language

Insight

Real history, plotted beautifully

Flux pulls your actual Home Assistant history and renders smooth, multi-entity charts with live readouts — temperature, energy, solar, anything numeric — over the range you choose.

  • Live values and flexible time windows
  • Full metric / imperial unit conversion everywhere
  • Gauges, energy flow, and weather tiles too
Automate

Automations that branch — and explain themselves

A visual “When → Only if → Do” builder that reads like plain English, with branching Cases for different situations. Then test it, run it, and trace exactly where it succeeded or failed.

  • Triggers, conditions, and a deep action library
  • Nestable Cases — do different things in different situations
  • Built-in testing, monitoring, and step-by-step traces
